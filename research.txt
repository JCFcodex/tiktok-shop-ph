Apply to RESEARCH.

SOURCES (in this order)
1. [S] itself: read everything visible, including text
   printed on the product in its images.
2. The official brand or manufacturer page.
3. The TikTok Shop listing (if I give a link or you can
   find it).
4. Other listings of the SAME product and variant.
- Same product only: match brand, product name, variant
  (shade, model) and size. Never mix in another variant or a
  similar product.
- If sources conflict, show both, tag UNVERIFIED and ask me.
  Never pick one silently.
- No web access, or a link you can't open: say so, work from
  [S] only and tag the rest UNVERIFIED. Never pretend to
  have checked a source.

PRODUCT SHEET (your reply to RESEARCH)
One sheet per item (MULTI-PRODUCT: ITEM 1, ITEM 2… in the
order they appear; they map to [A1], [A2]… in the order I
send them).
1. ID: brand · product name · the variant [S] shows · type ·
   what the listing includes (pieces/contents)
2. PRINTED TEXT (most important): ALL text printed on the
   product, including small print, on the ITEM itself and on
   its BOX/PACKAGING, exactly as printed: spelling,
   capitalization, numbers, symbols, language. Never
   translate, correct or guess. One line per place:
   - ITEM: front · back · sides · cap/lid/applicator · bottom
   - BOX/PACKAGING: front · back · sides · top/bottom ·
     inner, seal or sticker
   Tag every line: [SEEN] read directly from an image ·
   [FOUND: source] from a researched source · [UNVERIFIED]
   unclear, partial or conflicting. Example:
     BOX front: "BRAND" / "Product Name 50ml" [SEEN]
     ITEM back: "Net wt. 30 g" [FOUND: brand site]
   State plainly when a part has no text or there is no box.
3. SIZE (that variant): dimensions, volume, net weight and
   size options as stated, with the source.
4. COLOR (that variant): color or shade names as listed,
   finish and material, as stated and as seen.
5. GAPS: everything not found or UNVERIFIED, and the clearer
   photo that would settle it (example: back of the box).
Close with the next step (GENERATE) and anything NEEDED that
is still missing.
