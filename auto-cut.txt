AUTO-CUT (automatic: N = how many SHOTS I have)
Trigger: N = my SHOTS, each image counting as 1 however
many products it holds (example: 3 [R] = 3 SHOTS). N of 2
or more starts AUTO-CUT with no extra command; fewer is a
SINGLE SHOT. The video is auto-edited: one shot per image,
ALL of them, none skipped, joined by N-1 HARD JUMP CUTS,
totalling EXACTLY 10.0 seconds.
- Before the prompts, say in one line: N, the shot lengths
  and the cut times.
- Order: the order of the SHOTS, unless I say otherwise.
- Each shot is built from its own image (set by the
  command): Shot 1 = SHOTS image 1 = the starting frame,
  and so on.
- One image per shot, FRONT-ONLY, ONE simple move so each
  stays accurate and readable. Nothing from another shot
  ever appears.
- Hard jump cut = an instant swap on the exact timestamp. No
  fades, dissolves, wipes, slides, zooms, whip pans, flash
  cuts, morphing or slow motion. These planned cuts are the
  only cuts allowed, and only the product changes at each.
- Shot lengths (always 10.0s total):
    2 shots: 5.0 + 5.0 (cut at 5.0)
    3 shots: 3.5 + 3.5 + 3.0 (cuts at 3.5, 7.0)
    4 shots: 2.5 x 4 (cuts at 2.5, 5.0, 7.5)
    5 shots: 2.0 x 5 (cuts at 2.0, 4.0, 6.0, 8.0)
    6 shots: 2.0 + 2.0 + 1.5 x 4
             (cuts at 2.0, 4.0, 5.5, 7.0, 8.5)
  More than 6: split evenly into the fewest videos of up to
  6 shots (example: 8 = 4 + 4), each with its own two
  prompts, unless I say pick 6. Shots under 1.5s are too
  short to stay accurate.
- If the tool can't make exactly 10s, say so and scale every
  shot proportionally.
- Write each of the two prompts as a timeline (example for
  3 shots; reference = the [A] of each product in that
  shot, reused across shots; <image> and <one simple move>
  are set by the command):
    10-second 9:16 realistic smartphone video made of 3 shots
    joined by 2 hard jump cuts, no other transitions.
    SHOT 1 (0.0–3.5s): <image> 1
    (reference A1: <plain shape description>), FRONT ONLY,
    <one simple move>.
    HARD CUT at 3.5s.
    SHOT 2 (3.5–7.0s): <image> 2
    (reference A2: ...), FRONT ONLY, <one simple move>.
    HARD CUT at 7.0s.
    SHOT 3 (7.0–10.0s): <image> 3
    (reference A3: ...), FRONT ONLY, <one simple move>.
    Same in every shot: background [C] and hands [B]; each
    shot keeps its own image's camera angle, framing and
    lighting.
