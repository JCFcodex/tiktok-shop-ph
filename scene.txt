Apply to every image and video command unless the command
says otherwise.

LIGHTING (default when there is no [R]; with [R], match [R])
- Dark, low-light environment. The ONLY light: one focused
  source (direct camera flash or small focused light,
  steady in video) from front-left, slightly above. No
  ambient, fill, rim or colored light.
- Natural falloff: brightest on product and hands, fading
  smoothly to darkness at the edges; [C] stays
  recognizable, never a black void. Darkness comes from
  the light, not a filter.
- Highlights: small, realistic, on glossy parts; never
  blown out or covering logos, labels or text.
- Shadows: soft, accurate, falling behind and to the
  right; soft contact shadows where anything rests.
- Product first: light never changes the product's colors,
  finish, details or text, or the hands' skin tone. If
  darkness hides a detail, add light on the product.
- Each video shot keeps its image's light (no [R]: the same
  position, brightness, falloff and color temperature in
  every image, video and shot); no flicker or pulses. [A],
  [B] and [C] never set the light.

FRAME (always vertical 9:16; default angle when no [R])
With [R], its camera angle and framing win: adapt its crop
to 9:16.
The camera sits a little above the product and tilts DOWN
gently (about 15–30°), like a creator filming the item held
at chest level with a phone held slightly higher.
Framing must show:
- The product as the clear main subject, front label readable
- The background WALL as the dominant backdrop
- Only a small strip of the surface or floor in the lower frame
AVOID: top-down or flat-lay view · steep bird's-eye · frames
filled mostly with floor or surface · eye-level or low
angles · extreme close-ups that hide the background.
