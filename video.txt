SPECS
- Two ready-to-copy Google Flow prompts in separate code
  blocks, for my latest batch.
- Product identity: [A] (MULTI-PRODUCT: [A1], [A2]…) ·
  Background: [C] · Hands: [B].
- 10s if supported · each shot keeps its image's camera
  angle and framing (no tilting down to the floor, no
  angle changes, no zoom jumps).
- Each prompt must work alone in Google Flow, so it states
  in its own words LOCK (identical to [A] in every frame,
  true scale to the hands, never enlarged, shrunk,
  stretched or distorted), GRIP, LIGHTING, FRONT-ONLY and
  ANTI-GLITCH. Describe each product only as far as its own
  photo and the shot's image show.

FRONT-ONLY (FRONT = what the shot's image shows, from [R])
- Show ONLY what the shot's image shows: the same
  products, each with the same face toward the camera, for
  the whole shot. Never any other side, back, bottom or
  inside, not even a sliver; nothing new turns toward the
  camera.
- No spinning, rotating, turning, flipping or turning the
  product over. No opening, twisting or unscrewing it.
- Keep it alive with small, smooth moves, never more than
  about 15° off-front: tilts toward and away from the
  camera, slight left-right sways, gentle lifts and lowers,
  short slides across the frame, small push-ins and
  pull-backs. (The hands make these moves while gripping
  the products, all moving together with the same grip and
  arrangement as the image.)

ANTI-GLITCH
No physically impossible motion, morphing, warping,
melting, stretching, shrinking, size or proportion drift,
flicker, texture crawl, ghosting, duplication, disappearing
objects, shape, color or label changes, misspelled text,
extra, missing or fused fingers, clipping through hands,
sudden grip changes, an open or flattened hand, the product
resting on the palm, background or lighting shifts
(brightness, direction, flash pulses), camera jumps,
unplanned cuts or transitions, any side or back the image
doesn't show appearing.
If a move risks distortion, don't make it: hold the product still.

SINGLE SHOT (N = 1, or I say "no cuts")
Write one continuous 10s shot with no cuts, FRONT-ONLY,
from SHOTS image 1.
