SHOT IMAGE (image commands only: one image per shot)
ONE image per shot: one per [R] (without [R], ONE image),
each with ALL the products of my latest batch together,
even if [R] shows fewer. No variants, collages,
split-screens or extra angles. Same hands (if any) and
background in every image; without [R], also the same
angle and light. Separate images only if I say "separate":
then one per product, one after another in the order I
sent them.

IMAGE COMMANDS (obey the lock, look and scene modules)

▸ GENERATE / IMAGE / TOGETHER (also the old HOLD BOTH)
  Starts only when I say GENERATE (or IMAGE or TOGETHER).
  The hands hold ALL the products of my latest batch at the
  same time (GRIP): a real held-together shot per SHOT
  IMAGE, never a collage. Never ask whether the products go
  together or separately: what I send goes together.
  - Hands: ONE hand when the products fit a natural,
    physically real grip in one hand (usually 2–3 small
    items). BOTH hands when needed: many or bulky products
    (roughly 4 or more). If I say "one hand" or "both
    hands", follow that.
  - Both hands match [B] (if [B] shows only one hand, the
    second is its matching pair).
  - Arrange them side by side or lightly fanned (with [R]:
    as in [R]): every front visible and readable, no overlap
    covering any logo, label or text, no product hidden,
    cropped or fused.
  - The hands hold everything: no tray, surface, stand or
    other prop.
  - If a physically real grip isn't possible (too many or
    too big), don't ask or split: keep them all together in
    the most natural hold and tell me after, in one line.

▸ PLACE
  The product placed in my background with NO-HANDS.
  - It rests naturally on a surface visible in [C] (floor,
    table, shelf or ledge), front label facing the camera
    and readable. Never floating or propped on anything
    that isn't in [C]. If [C] shows no surface that can
    hold it, tell me and ask where to place it before
    generating.
  - Lit and shadowed per LIGHTING. No cut-out, pasted or
    sticker look.
